# The question set

Generated from `network.json`. Every position gets the same five questions, so a dossier can be judged against a standard rather than against taste. A dossier is DONE when all five are answered with sources that are about the world.

## The five questions

1. **Base rate.** What has happened in analogous cases? Name the reference class and say why it is the right one.
2. **Mechanism.** What would have to be true for this position to obtain? Name the causal chain, and the step in it that is weakest.
3. **Current evidence.** What does the record as of 2026 actually show? Figures, dated.
4. **Resolution criteria.** What observation in what year would settle whether this happened? A prior nobody can score is not a forecast.
5. **What would move it.** Which observable, if it arrived, should raise or lower this prior, and by roughly how much?

## Standard of evidence

A source counts if it is **about the world**: measured data, a dated record, a named institution's stated plan, a peer-reviewed result, a market price, a filing. Another forecaster's scenario counts as **one** input among several and never as the base rate. The present priors are a reading of four scenario documents; the point of this programme is to put them on evidence.

## Interactions to establish

The model carries 14 conditional stories. Each of these pairs is either already claimed and needs evidence, or is plainly real and unclaimed:

- **T × C** — Does tempo determine what coordination is possible? Claimed. Needs evidence.
- **T × A** — Does speed degrade oversight? Claimed. Needs a mechanism and a rate.
- **T × D** — Does capability speed change diffusion speed? UNCLAIMED — D has no parent.
- **E × D** — Does displacement drive the demand crisis? Claimed via one mechanism.
- **E × S** — Does a capex bust constrain compute? Claimed.
- **C × S** — Do coordination regimes shape where compute sits? Claimed.
- **D × P** — Does displacement drive backlash? Claimed. Needs a base rate from past shocks.
- **S × T** — Does compute supply gate capability? UNCLAIMED and load-bearing.
- **P × C** — Does public opinion change what governments can sign? UNCLAIMED.
- **A × C** — Does a safety failure make a deal harder or easier? Claimed, one direction only.
