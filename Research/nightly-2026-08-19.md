The last one is the sharpest. The sheet told a reader the model asserts an independence the
evidence denies, and named the four gaps — and the parent had closed all four in the rebuild. The
paragraph read as the most checkable thing on the plate.

**The general form.** A letter inherits a new MEANING and is wrong about one row. A quantifier —
*all*, *every*, *each*, *no* — inherits a new DOMAIN and is wrong about every member the rebuild
added. A dated statement of what was measured survives a rebuild; a present-tense universal cannot,
because the set it ranges over is read at the moment a reader reads it.
