Eighteen new wiki models arrived over forty-one days. **Not one of them matched.** `unmatched_wiki`

The reason is on disk. `data/witness/epoch-notable-models.csv` has 1,041 rows, its most recent

**Fourteen of the wiki's 74 models are dated after the snapshot's last row.** They cannot match,
whatever the matcher does; the ceiling on `matched` is 60 of 74 and falls further every time the
wiki adds a current model. `date_disagreements` (4) and `epoch_frontier_gap_count` (133) are pinned
by the same frozen file. Three of the witness's four reported numbers are properties of a snapshot,
not of the world.

And the gate ratchets the one that cannot fall. `audit_all.py:286` — *"matched count may not
fall"* — compares 20 against 20 and passes, as it has 38 times. **The match rate fell 35.71% →
27.03%, a 24.3% relative erosion in the witness's coverage, and not one of the six ratchet paths
can see it**, because the only witness quantities the gate reads are counts and the population they
are drawn from is not among them.

This is `review.pct` inverted. Both defects have one root: **the ratchet compares a number against
its own past without holding the population fixed.** `review.pct` chose a denominator that grows,
so it tightens on arrivals. `witness_epoch.matched` chose no denominator at all, so it cannot
register a fall. A count and a rate are not two styles of the same check; each is blind in the
direction the other sees.

**Three of six ratchet paths have never moved.** That is this project's own standing rule turned on

reasons. `explicit_pct` is frozen at the day-one 91.7 because the ratchet only tightens on
improvement and the live share has been **below** the baseline for the whole recorded history — it
is a floor that has never once been raised. The two witness counts are frozen because their
reference is.
