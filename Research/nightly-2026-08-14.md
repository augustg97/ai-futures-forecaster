## The defect this run found and fixed

`marginals30()` indexed the history by ROW — `h[h.length - 31]` — and clamped to `h[0]` when
the history was shorter. The history is 16 rows spanning 14 days. So the lookback landed on
2026-07-31 and **every label still read "in 30 days"**: the axis-note drift on all seven axes,
the board heading `WEIGHT AND ITS 30-DAY DRIFT`, and the capability-tempo caption "the reading
stood thirty days ago". A 14-day comparison, lettered as 30, on the largest instrument on the
sheet. It has read that way on every build since the project started.

This is the standing rule broken in its own house: *never draw something plausible when data is
missing*. The 30-day comparison did not exist and the sheet drew one anyway.

**Fixed** in `web/js/app.js`, `sections.js`, `instruments.js` — the lookback is now picked **by
date**, and every caller states the span it actually measured:

- axis notes → `(+3.3pp in 14 days)`
- board heading → `CAPABILITY TEMPO · WEIGHT AND ITS 14-DAY DRIFT`
- dial caption → `the reading stood 14 days ago`
- conditioned view (`cond` set, no lookback) → one needle, and the caption says so

Picking by date also repairs a defect that would have outlived the short history. The history
carries **a duplicated date — `2026-08-13` appears twice**, because the r3 registry re-set wrote
a second row for that day. Row-count indexing would therefore have reported "30 days" while
measuring 29, permanently, once the history got long enough to stop clamping. Checked against a
synthetic 46-row history with one duplicated date: the date method returns exactly 30, the row
method 29.

Verified against the live build by instrumenting `fillText` and reading the lettering: zero
occurrences of "30 days" or "thirty" outside the recession prose, `lookbackDays: 14` in sheet
state. Audit sweep on the published build: **23 cases · 15,421 marks · 0 collisions · 0
off-section · 0 overflows · `controlPasses: true`**.

stays here rather than going into the drawing. `delta.json` already splits `evidence` from
`grounding` per position in its `moved` field; a registry channel alongside them would let the
ghost needle stand for the world and the re-set be drawn as what it is.
