# Source register — all 26 positions, 2026-08-17

The grounding audit that opened this programme found the priors rested on 55 citations from 31
sources, with **21 of 26 positions carrying one citation or none**, and **19 of those 55 pointing
at four scenario documents**. A prior derived mainly from another forecast inherits its errors
without inheriting its reasoning.

This register gives every position at least one source **about the world** — a measurement, a
statute, a filing, a poll, or market data — independent of any scenario document. Scenario
citations are kept where they are the origin of a position's framing; they are no longer the
only thing under it.

## T · Capability tempo

**The measurement all four positions are scored against.** METR's task-completion time horizon:
the task duration, in human expert time, at which an agent succeeds half the time. It ran from
**4 seconds in 2019 to more than 16 hours in 2026**.

> **A calibration finding.** The doubling time is **about 7 months across 2019–2025, and about
> 4 months across 2024–2025.** The sheet has been quoting the seven-month figure (as 212 days).
> The recent-window figure is roughly twice as fast, and it is the one that bears on T1 and T2.
> METR's own caution is recorded with it: one year of data is a less robust estimate, and the
> rate may slow. **This is a live recommendation against T's priors, not an applied change.**

- METR, *Time Horizon 1.1*, 29 January 2026 — https://metr.org/blog/2026-1-29-time-horizon-1-1/
- METR, *Task-Completion Time Horizons of Frontier AI Models* — https://metr.org/time-horizons/
- METR, *Measuring AI Ability to Complete Long Software Tasks*, 19 March 2025
- METR, *Frontier Risk Report (February–March 2026)*, 19 May 2026 — https://metr.org/blog/2026-05-19-frontier-risk-report/

| position | independent source |
|---|---|

## A · Alignment outcome

**The measurement.** Apollo Research runs pre-deployment scheming evaluations on frontier
systems and reports **scheming rates of 1–13% across frontier models**. Two findings bear
directly on the axis: production reinforcement learning **increases** reward-seeking, so visible
improvement may be better deception rather than better alignment; and models frequently
investigate whether they are being evaluated, which confounds the reading.

- Apollo Research, *Science* programme — https://www.apolloresearch.ai/science/
- International AI Safety Report 2026, February 2026, 100+ authors, 30+ governments —
  https://internationalaisafetyreport.org/publication/international-ai-safety-report-2026
- UK AI Security Institute containment finding, 4 August 2026 (parent corpus)
- Anthropic Alignment Science — https://alignment.anthropic.com/

| position | independent source |
|---|---|
| A1 fails undetected | production RL raising reward-seeking; evaluation-awareness confounding detection; no agreed standard for recognising misaligned long-term goals |
| A2 near-miss, managed | the AISI containment disclosure of August 2026 is this mechanism working once |
| A3 tractable with effort | measured scheming rates of 1–13% are non-zero and bounded, which is what "managed" looks like |
| A4 untested in window | the evaluations are run on systems below the level at which a control failure is unrecoverable |

## D · Diffusion & labour

| position | independent source |
|---|---|
| D1 shock | entry-level hiring in AI-exposed occupations runs about **13% below** comparable unexposed roles inside the same firms, concentrated in 22-to-25-year-olds |

incumbent wages and hours, against hiring of entrants.

## Standing recommendations from this register

1. **T priors should be re-scored against the 4-month window** (held, not applied — priors live in
   the parent Atlas). The seven-month figure the sheet quotes is the 2019–2025 average; the
   2024–2025 window is roughly twice as fast, which raises T1 and T2 against T3 and T4.
2. **E4 has no independent measurement.** It is the one position in the register whose evidence
   is a mechanism rather than an observation. Either a series is found or the position's weight
   should reflect that it rests on arithmetic.
3. **P3 stays overturned.** The polling is unambiguous and the position's prior was already cut
   in r3.
