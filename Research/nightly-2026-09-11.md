## A shape check that caught itself

The first reader written tonight took `events.json` as a list and got 2,581 rows with a `fell_back`
key on each, which is what a correct read looks like. Pointed at the file's actual top level — a
one-key dict wrapping `events` — the same expression yielded the string `"events"` and the count
1. It raised `AttributeError` on the first row. It is the `read_headcl` family, and it failed loudly
only because the fallback in the expression happened to fire on one path and not the other. Every
reader in this note asserts its shape before counting.
