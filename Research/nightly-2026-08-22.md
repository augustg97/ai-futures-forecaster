C.C1   ev-export-retaliation 08-21   +0.960pp     |  three ev-enforcement-action firings: −0.159pp

It is 94.7% of the net S3 move and 120% of the net C1 move — larger than the whole rest of the
board's motion on that axis, and pointed the other way. Its driver is House ranking members
**asking the Commerce Secretary to explain** export-control policy; it raised *Unilateral
controls* by nearly a point.

The magnitude is set by the impact class and the repeat damping, and the direction is set by the
rule. Neither is set by the event. This firing carried `magnitude 0.008` against a window median
of `0.00045` — **17.7× the median, 2.7× the next largest** — and `repeat_k 0.0`, so the r2 novelty
floor damped it not at all, while `ev-capital-commitment` sits at `k ≈ 15` after nineteen firings.

magnitude**. Total absolute position motion contributed over the window, by rule:
