**The numerator starts at 175 and ends at 175.** Over the sixteen days from 2026-08-29 it never
left `[173, 175]` — a band of two on a base of 175 — while the denominator took on 263 events. The
rate fell on **all thirteen** transitions in that window, each fall ratcheted the floor down, and
**not one of those thirteen improvements came from reviewing an item.**

The cost is arithmetic, not rhetoric. The gate refuses at `pct > floor + 0.5`. On tonight's
ratcheted floor of 6.690 that is 189 items. On 08-29's 7.437 it would have been 208. **Sixteen
days of dilution cut the review headroom from 33 items to 14 — better than halved — and bought
nothing with it.** The first batch that flags thirty events for review now refuses the build, and
the reason it refuses will be that corpus growth spent a tolerance meant to absorb variation in
the numerator.

The rule this belongs under: **ratchet a rate against its own numerator's history, or the
denominator spends the tolerance.** Same family as every defect this project has found — a
surface whose meaning depends on something it does not show. `review.pct` shows a number falling
and means a corpus growing.
