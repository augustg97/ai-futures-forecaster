## The defect this run found and fixed

The morning plate is the surface whose entire job is to let a reader check an application against
the development that drove it. It was lettering **the parent's internal position keys**:

```
A.A3 +0.04pp
DRIVER: Anthropic published a redacted risk report on August 14, 2026 raising its
        estimate of the risk of misalignment in high-stakes situations fro …
```

A3 is **"tractable with effort"**. A1, moved down, is **"fails undetected"**. So the sheet drew a
lab raising its own misalignment risk estimate, and beside it moved the model toward alignment
being tractable — and no reader could see it, because the two numbers that would have made the
contradiction visible were written in keys the drawing never expands.

**Fixed** in `web/js/app.js`: each position now letters what it means beside its arithmetic, from
the registry the parent emitted, so a new axis or position carries its own name with no edit here.
The row now reads `A.A3 +0.04pp · tractable with effort`.

Two further fixes, both found while verifying the first:

- **The plate held three rows whatever the morning brought.** Six arrived; three sat below the
  fold. The plate said so — the fold note is honest — but the two applications it dropped were
  exactly the two whose direction contradicts their own driver. `morning.height` now grows with
  the day's count to a cap of 8, past which the fold note carries the remainder.
- **`column()` took `wrap()`'s first line and dropped the rest**, silently. The sight glasses read
  `D · DIFFUSION &` and `C ·` — one of the seven naming nothing at all. The label now shrinks to
  fit and, past the floor, marks its own cut: `A · ALIGNMENT OUTC…`. `column()` has one caller, so
  the change is contained.

Verified against the live build by instrumenting `fillText`: six rows drawn, ten position names
lettered, no fold note, all seven glasses named. Audit sweep on the published build: **23 cases ·
16,019 marks · 0 collisions · 0 off-section · 0 overflows · `controlPasses: true`**.

## Held, not applied — [G13](findings/gaps.md)

the finding points. A rule keyed on "safety research was published" reads a rising risk estimate
as evidence that safety research is working.

from the finding itself, or a symmetric widening of A — raising its uncertainty without moving its
centre — since a safety finding appearing is real information about attention and none about
outcome. Recorded as G13. **No prior was touched; priors live in the Atlas.**
