### The alternative is not a proposal; it already ran for thirteen nights

Last night's held remedy was to draw the argmax and letter the medoid beside it. The archive
contains that rule as a control, because it is what the sheet did before 2026-09-03: the drawn
world-line on 08-20 through 09-01 is the current argmax vector byte for byte, and the 09-01 drawn
`p` of 7.524e-06 is the 09-03 argmax `p` to every digit.

```
                          nights  transitions  marginals moved  total pp  line changed  ledger changed
argmax  08-20 .. 09-01      13         12            12          7.0950        0             0
medoid  09-03 .. 09-12       9          8             8          5.3203        4             4
```

This sharpens the recommendation and corrects its framing. The argmax rule does remove the noise:
7.1 pp of probability mass moved across twelve transitions and the drawn line never moved once.
But that is the whole of what it buys. **Its chronicle was frozen for thirteen consecutive nights
while the marginals moved on every one of them** — the same eleven entries at the same years, from
the first night G existed to the last night before the medoid. Under either rule the ledger and
the evidence are uncorrelated: the medoid answers at random on axes nothing wrote, and the argmax
does not answer at all.

So the case for the swap stands and its terms change. Drawing the argmax makes the sheet stop
reporting motion that did not happen. It does not make the sheet report motion that did. A reader
who wants the night's news has the delta plate, which is exact and says so; the chronicle is a
portrait of one world-line and should be lettered as one, with neither rule pretending it is a
bulletin.

**Held, not applied.** `mainline.kind` is the parent's and standing rule 2 governs. Recorded for
August, with the control now measured rather than argued.
