# Nightly 2026-08-17 — the parent rebuilt its registry, and the sheet held

**Outcome: not published.** The live sheet still serves build `20260817-0020`, drawn against
`r4-2026-08-17`. That build is internally coherent — r4 data under r4-keyed prose — and it stays
up until the authored layer is re-keyed.

The Atlas gate **passed** (baselines hold, 2,131 events, 91.4% explicit against an 81.5% target).
Nothing is wrong with the parent's data. What moved is the shape of the registry it emits.

## What the parent emitted

`network.json` went from `r4-2026-08-17` (dated 08-16) to **`r5-2026-08-17`**: 7 axes and 26
positions became **9 axes and 49 positions**.

Two axes are new:

| axis | name | positions |
|------|------|-----------|
| K | Takeoff shape | K1 rungs inside one year · K2 one to two years · K3 two to five years · K4 gap past five years |
| R | Regulatory architecture | R1 developer commitments · R2 contested patchwork · R3 federal preemption · R4 executive release gate · R5 civil regime enforced · R6 written and deferred |

And **all 26 positions that kept their letter changed their meaning.** Four of them changed it to
something close to the opposite:

| position | r4 | r5 |
|----------|----|----|
| P1 | populist backlash | acquiescence through use *(backlash is now P5)* |
| D1 | shock | delivery stalls *(the shock is now D4)* |
| E2 | bubble corrects, build-out survives | margin squeeze *(the correction is now E3)* |
| A3 | tractable with effort | catch buys pause |

T4 moved from "continuous-normal (no SC in window)" to the dated band "2037 to 2050", so a
world-line labelled T4 no longer says a superintelligence is absent — it says it arrives late.

## What broke, and what would have broken quietly

**The climate extractor stopped, correctly.** `int_decline` in the parent's `worldlines.py` is now
keyed `S1…S5` against the `{S1,S2,S3}` the extractor asserted, and the bonus condition moved from
`C3` to `C4/C5`. The key check caught the first. **The second would not have been caught**: had the
map still been three-keyed, the build would have gone on subtracting the bonus on C3, which under
r5 means "declaratory accord" and no longer names the coordinated build-out the coupling is about.
A wrong emissions curve, computed confidently, drawn without a mark.

**The laws recorder would have drawn on NaN.** `engine.json` now emits `LAWS_RATE` keyed `R1…R6`;
`tracksJS` reads `P.LAWS_RATE[wl.C]`. A C-key against an R-map is `undefined`, and `laws + undefined`
is NaN for every year of every world-line.

**The prose would have said the wrong thing in the largest lettering on the sheet.** `narrative.js`
holds no K or R rows at all, lists the seven old axes in three places, and keys every FRAG, CROSS,
ECON and ECON_MOD row on position letters whose meanings have all moved. `figures.js` carries eight
position literals (C1 C3 C4 C5, E3 E4, P1 P3), `app.js` fourteen. None of this fails. It composes.
A P1 world-line would have been lettered with the backlash paragraph while the registry now means
acquiescence — the same defect this project has found four times: **a surface that renders its
source's internal representation instead of its meaning cannot be checked.**

## What was changed tonight

1. **`climate_params()` names no axis and no position.** It reads the axis the decline map is
   indexed by out of the parent's source, reads the positions that earn the bonus out of the
   condition itself (`in (…)` or `== "…"`), and checks both against the registry the parent emitted
   in the same run. A map that does not cover its axis is now fatal with the two sets printed.
   `climate.json` gained `decline_axis` and a `bonus: {axis, positions, amount}` object; `app.js`
   reads them. The `c3_bonus` field is gone, and with it the last mirrored literal in the climate
   path.

2. **A coverage gate.** `web/data/registry-covered.json` is the drawing's own declaration of which
   axes, positions and **meanings** its authored strings are keyed to. The build compares the pulled
   registry against it and refuses to publish on any difference, printing the diff above. Positive
   control run: the gate passes when the declaration matches the registry, so tonight's refusal is
   a measurement rather than a stuck red light. Wire new positions and restate the declaration in
   the same commit as the prose.

3. **The exit codes say what happened.** Every failure in `build_site.py` exited 1, so an unattended
   run was told "the Atlas gate refused" while the gate had passed — a diagnosis pointing at another
   project entirely. Now: 1 the gate refused · 2 pull or extractor · 3 push or verify · **4 the
   registry moved past the drawing**. `nightly.sh` passes the code through instead of collapsing it.
   Tonight's run exits 4.

## Note

The parent's `tracks()` is the second place this project has been bitten by reading a neighbour's
internal representation. The climate constants are extracted because mirroring them by hand was
judged too fragile; the six couplings around them were mirrored by hand anyway, and they are what
broke. The line between "a constant worth extracting" and "a condition worth mirroring" did not
survive a registry rebuild.
