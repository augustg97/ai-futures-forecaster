emitted `network.json` against the last committed copy — axes, position keys per axis, sub-axis
keys with their `origin` flag, and the conditional count — and prints what moved. It refuses
nothing; the coverage gate owns refusal and owns the right cases, since a sub-axis needs no
re-keying while a position that changed meaning does.
