# Interactions — synthesis after the first round of dossiers

The model carries **14 conditional stories**. Seven dossiers now give several of them evidence,
contradict one, and identify four edges that should exist and do not.

## Edges the evidence supports

| edge | model says | evidence |
|---|---|---|
| `C\|T1` | explosive tempo closes the deal window | supported — and **S** sharpens it: verification targets advanced packaging, in the disputed jurisdiction |
| `P\|D1` | displacement drives populist backlash | supported and **specified**: the measured effect is −13% entry-level hiring in exposed occupations, 22–25s only |
| `E\|D3` | slow diffusion starves the demand-crisis spiral | **confirmed by measurement** — four years on, the aggregate effect is undetectable, and E4 is correspondingly low |
| `S\|E3` | a capex bust strands build-outs | supported — and the *nearer* mechanism is the ~$176B depreciation gap, which is a balance-sheet event |

## Edges that should exist and do not

| edge | why | source |
|---|---|---|
| **`T\|S`** | three of Epoch's four constraints on continued scaling **are** the S axis; T currently has no parent | G1 |
| **`D\|T`** | diffusion follows capability with a lag the exposure data can measure; D has no parent | G3 |
| **`D\|E`** | the 2022–24 entry-level decline is confounded by the Fed's policy shift — that confounder is E | G3 |
| **`P\|C`** | 57–19 opposition to federal preemption is opinion **acting on** what governments can enact | P dossier |

## The edge the evidence contradicts

`P` is modal at **P3 (polarised-fractured)**, whose own resolution criterion is a partisan
split. The 2026 polling shows **cross-party agreement on restriction** — 43% of Trump voters and
70% of Harris voters opposing preemption, 9:1 on child safety. The division is **elite vs
public**, not left vs right, and the model has no position for it.

## The design finding

Three axes now show the same fault (**G8**): they mix *magnitude* with *incidence* on one axis,
so a world where something is **sharp and narrow** is inexpressible.

- **S** — siting diversifying while chip supply concentrates
- **D** — entry-level hiring collapsing while aggregate employment is flat
- **P** — a public united against its own government

Each is the state the 2026 record actually shows, and in each case the model must round it to
whichever half is louder, then propagate the rounded state through every downstream variable.

**Proposed design rule: one axis, one question.** An axis answering "how big" and "where" at
once will always have a world it cannot say.
